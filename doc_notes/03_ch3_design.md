## 3.1 Philosophy, significance, objectives

### 1) ปรัชญาของหลักสูตร (verbatim)
> "มุ่งผลิตบัณฑิตวิศวกรรมโลจิสติกส์ที่มีความรู้และทักษะเชิงปฏิบัติ สามารถวิเคราะห์ ออกแบบ และพัฒนาระบบอุตสาหกรรมและโซ่อุปทานอย่างสร้างสรรค์ มีจรรยาบรรณวิชาชีพ ความรับผิดชอบต่อสังคมและสิ่งแวดล้อม และเรียนรู้ตลอดชีวิตเพื่อการพัฒนาอย่างยั่งยืน"

Gist: the program aims to produce logistics engineers with practical knowledge and skills who can analyse, design and develop industrial and supply-chain systems creatively. Graduates should follow professional ethics, act responsibly toward society and the environment, and keep learning for sustainable development.

### 2) ความสำคัญของหลักสูตร (5 paragraphs, summarised)
1. Production and logistics systems are increasingly linked across the organisation and the whole supply chain. Managing resources, production planning, storage, transport and distribution needs systems-level analysis to balance cost, quality, time, service level and safety. Digital technology, automation and data analytics are moving the engineer's role from narrow operations to integrated system design and improvement.
2. The program builds expertise through **two complementary majors (สองวิชาเอก)**:
   - **วิชาเอกวิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ** covers design of production systems and work processes, investigation and analysis of engineering problems, engineering tools and models, project management, and innovation for operational efficiency.
   - **วิชาเอกวิศวกรรมการจัดการโซ่อุปทาน** covers integrated supply-chain design, planning and improvement of transport, warehousing and distribution, data analysis with digital technology, and sustainable supply-chain improvement proposals.
3. Competence rests on math, science and engineering fundamentals, plus systems thinking, problem investigation, experimental design, data collection and analysis, and choosing appropriate tools. Students learn to use quantitative evidence, judge the reliability and limits of analyses, and weigh feasibility under resource, budget, time and risk constraints.
4. The program stresses economic, social and environmental responsibility, law, standards and professional ethics, plus risk and impact assessment. Learning is linked to real problems of firms and communities, **especially manufacturing and agricultural-product supply chains in จังหวัดกาฬสินธุ์ and ภาคตะวันออกเฉียงเหนือ**.
5. The program follows the university's educational philosophy **"ประสบการณ์สร้างการเรียนรู้สู่มืออาชีพ"**. It integrates theory and practice through real-situation problem solving, engineering projects and **CWIE (Cooperative and Work Integrated Education)**, and promotes Thai and English communication, teamwork and self-directed learning. It uses **OBE (Outcome-Based Education)** as the design and assessment framework. The graduate identity (อัตลักษณ์) is **"มุ่งมั่น สร้างสรรค์ เชี่ยวชาญวิชาชีพ"**.

### 3) วัตถุประสงค์ของหลักสูตร (5 objectives)
1. Knowledge of science, math and engineering fundamentals, **plus specialised knowledge in วิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ and วิศวกรรมโลจิสติกส์**, applied to professional practice.
2. Analytical thinking, problem solving and efficient engineering system development, considering economic, social and environmental factors.
3. Applying digital technology, tools and innovation to raise industrial and logistics operational efficiency.
4. Thai and English communication, teamwork, and adapting to technological and professional change.
5. Morality, ethics, professional codes of conduct (จรรยาบรรณวิชาชีพ), social responsibility, and awareness of safety and sustainable development.

## 3.2 Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)

Stated count: **"หลักสูตรกำหนดผลลัพธ์การเรียนรู้จำนวน 13 ข้อ"**. This is **4 common PLOs** taken by both majors, plus **5 IE-specific** or **4 SC-specific** PLOs.
- So an IE student is assessed on 9 PLOs and an SC student on 8.
- There are no sub-PLOs, no Bloom levels and no domain codes. The English label for each PLO is given in brackets: [ ] for the common PLOs, ( ) for the major PLOs.

## YLOs (Yearly Learning Outcomes)

**Year 1 summary:** apply basic math, science and engineering to introductory calculation and analysis. Analyse linkages among industrial, logistics and supply-chain activities. Do engineering hand-skill workshop practice, use quality-inspection tools, analyse defects from real data, and identify safe improvement opportunities.

| YLO | Gist (Thai key phrase) | Supports |
|---|---|---|
| IE-YLO1.1 | ประยุกต์ใช้ความรู้ทางคณิตศาสตร์ วิทยาศาสตร์ และวิศวกรรมพื้นฐานในการคำนวณและวิเคราะห์ปัญหาทางวิศวกรรมเบื้องต้น | PLO_LE1 |

## Issues / inconsistencies

1. **Section numbering gap in 3.2.** It has "1) ผลลัพธ์การเรียนรู้ระดับหลักสูตร" followed directly by "2.1 / 2.2 / 2.3". There is no "2) ผลลัพธ์การเรียนรู้รายปี" heading, and the numbering style switches from "1)" to "2.1".
2. **Missing OBE elements usually expected at this point.** The chapter has no Bloom taxonomy level per PLO, no domain tagging (TQF 2565 ความรู้/ทักษะ/จริยธรรม/ลักษณะบุคคล, or K/S/A), and no sub-PLOs. These may be in another chapter, but 3.2 does not present them.
3. **No สภาวิศวกร / engineering accreditation reference** anywhere in the chapter, although this is a วศ.บ. program. Washington Accord, ABET and QAA appear only as "supporting sources" inside the 3.4 cells. There is no PLO-vs-WA/ABET mapping table, even though the PLO English labels (Engineering Design, Investigation, Modern Tools, Project Management) mirror WA and ABET attributes.
4. **The stakeholder data-collection method is absent.** Table 1 names groups but gives no survey or interview method, n, response rate or dates. Groups named in 3.4 (ผู้ปกครอง, TPQI, ESCO, O*NET, Jobsdb, Washington Accord, ABET, QAA) do not appear in Table 1, which uses generic phrases such as "มาตรฐานสมรรถนะทางวิศวกรรม". So the two parts of the chapter are inconsistent about sources.
5. **Leaked working-note code "N13"** in 3.4 Year 3. It is the only need with an N-code, which implies an internal needs list (N1…). The repeated phrase "ที่มา: ความต้องการที่กลั่นกรองจากแหล่งข้อมูล" inside table cells also reads like drafting notes rather than final text.
6. **Hedging and drafting-reviewer language left in official tables:**
   - Table 2, PLO_IE2: "โดยไม่อ้างผลกระทบเชิงพื้นที่ที่ยังไม่มีหลักฐาน"
   - 3.3(3), PLO_IE5: "(เมื่อมีงานหรือหลักฐานเชิงพื้นที่รองรับ)"
   - 3.3(3), PLO_SC4: "(เมื่อมีหลักฐานการพัฒนาหรือผลกระทบในพื้นที่)"
   - 3.3(3), PLO_IE1: "เมื่อใช้โจทย์ออกแบบจากบริบทพื้นที่จริง"
   - 3.3(3), PLO_SC1: "เมื่อโจทย์อ้างอิงบริบทพื้นที่"
   - 3.3(3), PLO_IE4: "เชื่อมโยงเชิงสนับสนุน"
   These read as conditional or self-critical notes, not alignment statements.
7. **PLO wording differs between sections (Table 2 vs 3.2):**
   - PLO_IE1 in Table 2 reads "กระบวนการทางวิศวกรรม" (missing "อุตสาหการ").
   - PLO_IE3 in Table 2 reads "ปรับปรุงระบบ" (missing "อุตสาหการ").
   - PLO_IE4 in Table 2 reads "บริหารจัดการโครงการ" (missing "วิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ").
   - PLO_SC3 in 3.2 reads "การตัดสินใจพร้อมประเมิน" (no space), but "การตัดสินใจ พร้อม" elsewhere.
8. **The objectives and philosophy do not name the SC major.** Objective 1 says "ความรู้เฉพาะด้านวิศวกรรมอุตสาหการและวิศวกรรมโลจิสติกส์" rather than วิศวกรรมการจัดการโซ่อุปทาน. The philosophy and the Subject Specific definition refer generically to "วิศวกรรมโลจิสติกส์".
9. **Abbreviations for the SC major are inconsistent.** PLO and YLO codes use "SC", while 3.4 uses "SCME". The English major name is "Supply Chain Management Engineering".
10. **Formatting of the English labels is inconsistent.** Common PLOs use [ ] and major PLOs use ( ). In 3.3(3) only PLO_LE1 repeats its English label.
11. **Thin and late YLO coverage of the common PLOs:**
    - PLO_LE3 and PLO_LE4 have no YLO in Years 1–3 for either major. They appear only in Year 4 (YLO4.3, YLO4.4).
    - PLO_LE2 appears in Years 1–3 only for IE (IE-YLO1.3). The SC major has it only at YLO4.2.
    - All SC Year 1 YLOs map to PLO_LE1 only.
    - The Year 4 YLOs map only to the common PLOs, so no major PLO is assessed in Year 4, despite coop/CWIE and projects.
12. **Odd early mappings.** IE-YLO1.3 (a Year 1 workshop) supports PLO_IE5 (innovation from real firm or community problems), which is ambitious for Year 1. IE-YLO1.2 frames IE Year 1 in terms of logistics and supply-chain activities, which is fine for a logistics program but blurs the line between the majors.
13. **YLO style is inconsistent.** SC-YLO2.3, 3.x and Year 4 YLOs end with "...ได้", while IE YLOs do not.
14. **The YLO counts are unequal between majors.** IE Year 2 has 5 YLOs but SC Year 2 has 3. SC Year 2 has no YLO for SC2 except the overloaded SC-YLO2.3, which supports 3 PLOs.
15. **Typo.** "(PLO_LE2, PLO_LE3 และ PLO_LE4))" has a double closing parenthesis.
16. **The percentages count all 13 PLOs pooled.** Per-major splits (IE 6/9 = 66.67%, SC 5/8 = 62.50%) are not given. Reviewers may expect them because each student is only accountable for 9 or 8 PLOs.
17. **The PLO_LE2 classification as Generic is debatable.** The PLO is about law, safety and engineering ethics, which are subject-specific in engineering accreditation terms.
18. **Gaps in the extracurricular activities (3.4):**
    - No Year 1 activity.
    - PLO_LE2 and PLO_IE4 are not addressed.
    - "ผู้ปกครอง" appears as a source only here.
    - Range notation "PLO_IE1–PLO_IE2" and "PLO_SC1–PLO_SC4" is used.
19. **Vague policy citation.** "แผนส่งเสริมเศรษฐกิจดิจิทัล / Digital Economy" has no official title or year, unlike the other plans. No regional or provincial plan is cited (e.g., Northeast or Kalasin development plan), despite the emphasis on Kalasin and the agricultural supply chain. Verify the official titles and years of all cited plans against the source documents.
20. **The vision and mission are not quoted.** 3.3(3) lists "พันธกิจข้อ 1" and "ข้อ 2" with short paraphrases. The university vision statement itself is not reproduced, so the alignment cannot be checked from this chapter alone.
21. **DOCX extraction artifacts** (not errors in the source): line breaks mid-phrase in IE-YLO2.3 ("วิศวกรรม / อุตสาหการ"), in the SC Year 2 summary ("ระบบ / โลจิสติกส์"), and in PLO_IE1 in 3.3(4) ("ตลอด / วัฏจักรชีวิต").
