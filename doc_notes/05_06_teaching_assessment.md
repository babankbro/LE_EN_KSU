## หมวดที่ 5 กระบวนการจัดการเรียนการสอน

### 5.1 Constructive Alignment table (PLO → teaching method → aligned assessment)
Each PLO gets 2–3 teaching activities and 3 matching assessment artefacts. Highlights:
- **LE1**: calculation practice, Systems Thinking root-cause analysis, KPI/alternative comparison in transport/warehouse/projects → calculation exams with steps, relationship diagrams, cost/time/KPI reports.
- **LE2**: risk/safety case studies, impact analysis, ethics discussion "ก่อน/ระหว่างสหกิจ" → risk & legal-compliance forms, impact/sustainability report, ethics decision Rubric from simulation/co-op.
- **LE3**: Thai engineering reports, English vocabulary/reading/speaking/writing, role-assigned lab/project teams → Thai report/presentation forms, English speaking/writing or abstract, team role Rubric.
- **LE4**: skill-gap survey and goal setting, self-study of new tools, self-assessment from programming/GIS/project/co-op → individual learning plan and Gap Analysis, artefact using self-learned tech, Learning Log/Portfolio + Self-assessment.
- **IE1**: design briefs (work system/product/plant layout) → requirements analysis sheet, design output with Rubric, design comparison incl. cost/quality/risk and LCA.
- **IE2**: research question/variables/experiment plan, material/system testing, error & uncertainty discussion → experiment plan, lab checklist + real logbook, results report.
- **IE3**: model selection, OR and automation labs, sanity-checking models → method-selection sheet, LP/Assignment/Queue/Simulation models or automation results, Rubric incl. Sensitivity/limitations.
- **IE4**: production plan and plant-layout project plans (activities, owners, budget, schedule), simulated execution → plans, task-allocation evidence, KPI comparison report.
- **IE5**: stakeholder problem analysis, improvement proposals, technical/economic/sustainability feasibility → needs analysis, innovation draft/prototype, Feasibility / benefit–cost report.
- **SC1**: flow analysis procurement→distribution, SC design exercises, evaluate by cost/service/risk → linkage diagram, SC plan, cost/service/risk table.
- **SC2**: demand–inventory, transport and warehouse equipment planning, KPI calculation → quantitative plan, KPI results, before/after comparison.
- **SC3**: logistics data with digital tools/models, interpretation, data completeness checks → analysis files, Dashboard or recommendation report, data-quality Checklist.
- **SC4**: enterprise/community problem study, green logistics / asset proposals, 3-dimension feasibility → problem/stakeholder report, improvement proposal, Feasibility / 3-dimension impact evaluation.

#### 2) Growth Mindset (กรอบคิดแบบเติบโต)
Four principles: ความเชื่อในการพัฒนาได้ (pre/post problems, Scaffolding; คณิตศาสตร์วิศวกรรม 1, การโปรแกรมคอมพิวเตอร์), การมองความล้มเหลวเป็นโอกาส (Iteration, Debugging, After-action review; IE automation lab, programming), การเรียนรู้จากข้อเสนอแนะ (Formative Feedback, Peer-review, draft→revision with Change Log, Rubric CLO; project prep and project), การท้าทายตนเองอย่างต่อเนื่อง (Self-directed Learning, Learning Contract; data analytics, GIS, สหกิจศึกษา).

#### 3) Lifelong learning
Four dimensions: Self-Directed Learning (Google Classroom, online resources, data/GIS software), Professional Development (workshops, site visits, online courses/skill certificates, co-op enterprises), Knowledge Sharing (group discussion, Google Drive/Classroom, academic seminars), Innovation and Research (academic databases, modelling software, labs, field data tools).

#### 4) Theory-to-practice linkage (four levels)
- Course Level: CLO–activity–assessment linked only to PLOs the course is responsible for (e.g., GIS routing, EOQ-type inventory analysis, plant/warehouse layout).
- Project Level: project proposal first (การเตรียมโครงงาน), then project with Thai–English report/abstract; advisor + students + optional problem owner.
- Internship Level (WIL / co-op): สหกิจศึกษา preparation, then supervised placement; assessed by พนักงานที่ปรึกษา and อาจารย์นิเทศ; covers skill gaps, law, ethics, safety, collaboration; outputs: work report, stakeholder evaluations, self-development plan.
- Research Level: systematic experiments in IE major courses and/or analytical case studies in SC.

### 6.2 Assessment tools (Authentic Assessment Tools)
Eight tool types, all using a **4-level rubric** (except tool 8):
1. Written tests & case studies – Analytic Rubric 4 ระดับ (correctness, reasoning method, justification); LE1, IE1, IE3, SC1–SC4; end of year per YLO.
2. Practical tests & simulations (computer, lab, OR/PLC/GIS) – Performance Rubric 4 ระดับ (procedure, result, safety, limitations); LE1, IE2–IE3, SC2–SC3.
3. Design and project-proposal evaluation – Analytic Rubric 4 ระดับ (requirements, design, alternative evaluation); IE1, IE4–IE5, SC1, SC4.
4. Behaviour/real-work observation by อาจารย์นิเทศ/พี่เลี้ยง – Behaviorally Anchored Rubric 4 ระดับ; LE2, LE3; continuous during co-op.
5. Presentation & interview – Communication Rubric 4 ระดับ (content, language, structure, Q&A); LE1, LE3.
6. Portfolio & reflection – Portfolio Rubric 4 ระดับ (goals, application, evidence, reflection); LE4; "สรุปตาม YLO4.4".
7. Individual-in-team evaluation (checklist, role log, peer assessment) – individual 4-level rubric; LE3.
8. Self-assessment / reflection questionnaire – rating scale + descriptive analysis; **indirect evidence only, "ไม่ใช้ตัดสินบรรลุ PLO เพียงลำพัง"**, reported separately from direct scores.

Rubric level descriptors (what 1, 2, 3, 4 mean) are **not** given in this chunk; only the criterion dimensions above. Level 3 is the pass level.

### 6.6 ระบบทวนสอบผลสัมฤทธิ์ของนักศึกษา (verification)
Verifies alignment among PLO, YLO, assessment tools and actual scores; a verification committee samples evidence and rubric results **separately for both majors**. Four steps:
1. Preparation – programme chair / programme lecturers / course owners: semester and year-end verification plan, check PLO–YLO vs tools/Rubric, calibrate assessors (before semester and before YLO assessment).
2. Sampling – คณะกรรมการทวนสอบ: sample key courses/assessments covering both majors, **levels R/M**, and high–mid–low score bands; include individual evidence.
3. Verification – committee / assigned experts: check validity of tasks/criteria vs YLO–PLO, re-mark samples and compare assessors, **check PLO weights total 100% per major and all key criteria passed**, identify discrepancies (before finalising PLO attainment).
4. Reporting – summarise by YLO and PLO per major, review fails and reassessments, assign owners/deadlines, follow up exam/Rubric revisions; outputs: verification report, meeting minutes, CQI plan (end of semester/year, before next offering).
