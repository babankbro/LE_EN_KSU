Arithmetic check (done by hand):
- GE: 15 + 9 = 24 ✓
- วิชาชีพบังคับ: 15 + 24 = 39 ✓
- หมวดวิชาเฉพาะ: 14 + 25 + 39 + 11 + 3 = 92 ✓
- Total: 24 + 92 + 6 = 122 ✓

Per-major path: both majors total 122. The only difference is the 24-credit major-compulsory block: 10 courses for IE (7 × 3 + 3 × 1) and 8 courses for SCM (8 × 3). Everything else is shared. The professional elective is 3 credits (one course) from a pool of 20, with no rule tying elective groups to majors.

For reference, the อว. 2565 bachelor standard minimums as I recall them are total ≥120, GE ≥24, free electives ≥6, and specific ≥72. This structure meets them. Verify against the original ประกาศ.

## 5. Issues / inconsistencies

### 5.1 Structure and arithmetic
1. **All credit sums add up** (24/92/6 = 122; 14 + 25 + 39 + 11 + 3 = 92; each group's listed courses match its stated credits, using unique courses).
2. **4.1 table is under-specified:** 14 labels and 12 values. Sub-rows (1) วิชาบังคับเอกวิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ* and (2) วิชาบังคับเอกวิศวกรรมการจัดการโซ่อุปทาน have no credit values. 3. **Asterisk on "วิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ*"** has no footnote in this chunk. Also notable: a major named วิศวกรรมอุตสาหการ inside a วิศวกรรมโลจิสติกส์ degree. The degree/major naming should be checked against chapter 1 and the สภาวิศวกร licensing branch.
