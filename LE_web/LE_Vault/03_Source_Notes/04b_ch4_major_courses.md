## Issues / inconsistencies

### A. PLO mapping (critical)
1. **None of the 47 courses map their CLOs to PLOs in this chunk.** The CLO tables carry only the Bloom level. If the CLO→PLO mapping lives elsewhere (e.g. curriculum mapping matrix, ch.4 §4.x), it must be cross-checked. If it does not exist anywhere, every course lacks the required OBE alignment.

### B. Wrong or copied content
2. **EN-788-13012 กฎหมายและข้อกำหนดในงานโลจิสติกส์:** both CLOs are copied from EN-788-13013 (international logistics and multimodal). There is no CLO on law, contracts, compliance or ethics, even though the description is entirely legal.
3. **EN-788-13013 CLO1:** the Thai text is missing its verb ("ความเชื่อมโยงของ..." where "วิเคราะห์ความเชื่อมโยง..." is expected). The intact version appears in 13012 CLO1, which suggests a copy/paste accident between the two.
4. **EN-788-13015:** the English description is duplicated in one cell with no separator ("situationPrinciples"), and "situation" should be "situations".
5. Bloom level does not match the CLO verb:
   - 13016 CLO1 "classify, analyze, evaluate cost-effectiveness" is tagged Understand.
   - 14008 CLO2 "identify hazards, assess risk, verify compliance" is tagged Understand.
   - Minor cases: 13018 CLO4, 14001 CLO3 and 14018 CLO4 contain "evaluate reliability" (ประเมินความน่าเชื่อถือ) but are tagged Analyze. 13005 CLO1 "select methods" is tagged Apply (acceptable).

### C. Prerequisites to cross-check (codes not defined in this chunk)
6. **EN-710-11006** ความน่าจะเป็นและสถิติวิศวกรรม / Probability and Engineering Statistics — prerequisite for EN-788-13003 and EN-788-13006.
7. **EN-001-007** ความน่าจะเป็นและสถิติวิศวกรรม / Probability and Engineering Statistics — prerequisite for EN-788-14006. This is the **same course name under a different, old-format code**. It is almost certainly a stale code that should be EN-710-11006.
8. Prerequisites that are defined in this chunk: EN-788-13002 (→13007), EN-788-17001 (→17002), EN-788-12006 (→12007). They are consistent.

### D. Structure / grouping
9. **There are 4 elective groups but only 2 majors.** The groups are การจัดการโลจิสติกส์และโซ่อุปทาน, วิศวกรรมการขนส่ง, วิศวกรรมการจัดการอุตสาหกรรม and วิศวกรรมการผลิต. None is labelled with a major. By content, LSCM and Transport fit the SCM major and Ind. Mgmt and Manufacturing fit the IE major. Whether students may take any group, or must take the groups of their major, is not stated in this chunk; check the ch.3 structure table. Also, the group heading in the document is "กลุ่มวิชาการจัดการโลจิสติกส์และโซ่อุปทาน", not "การจัดการโลจิสติกส์".
10. **Project course codes:** EN-788-12006 and 12007 use the `12xxx` common-core series but are listed under กลุ่มวิชาเสริมสร้างประสบการณ์ในวิชาชีพ (with co-op 17001/17002, `17xxx`). The chunk does not say whether co-op and project are both required or are alternative tracks (6 cr co-op vs 3 cr project); cross-check the ch.3 credit structure.
11. **Major core balance:** IE core = 7×3 + 3×1 = 24 cr; SCM core = 8×3 = 24 cr. Credits balance, but the IE major gets three 1-credit labs (13008–13010) and the SCM major has none apart from 13018, which is a 3(2-2-5) practice course.
12. **EN-788-13018 ปฏิบัติการวิศวกรรมขนส่งและจราจร** (SCM core) is the practice course, while the theory course **EN-788-14006 วิศวกรรมขนส่งและจราจร** is only an elective, and there is no prerequisite link between them. The sequencing is questionable.
13. **Transport elective scope:** 14008–14010 (aviation SMS, civil aviation law with emphasis on aircraft maintenance licensing and airworthiness, maritime law with SOLAS/MARPOL/class surveys) are regulatory and operations courses. They have no engineering design content, and 14009 in particular reads like an aircraft-maintenance syllabus. Their fit with a logistics engineering degree is questionable.
14. **Content overlaps worth reviewing:**
    - 12002 vs 13017 vs 14002 (warehouse equipment and operations).
    - 13007 project-management content vs 14012.
    - 13010 pneumatics/hydraulics/PLC vs 14016.
    - 13013 (Incoterms, customs) vs 14003.
    - 13003 quality vs 14018 X-bar/R.
    - 12003 vs 13018 (transport data analysis).
15. **Thin CLO coverage:** 13012, 13013 and 13015 have only 2 CLOs each, so large parts of their descriptions (13013 documents/customs/insurance; 13015 forecasting/capacity/risk) are not assessed.

### E. Credit notation
16. Most notations are internally consistent using credit = L + P/2 and self-study = 2L + P/2: 3(3-0-6), 3(2-2-5), 1(1-0-2), 3(1-6-5). The 1-credit labs **1(0-3-2)** (13008, 13009, 13010) use 3 practice hours per credit, and their self-study of 2 does not follow the 2L + P/2 rule (it would be 1.5). The 1(0-3-2) notation is allowed under Thai credit rules but is inconsistent with the 2-hour/credit ratio used everywhere else in this chunk.
17. **17002 6(0-40-0):** the co-op notation gives a weekly placement load. It is conventional, but it cannot be checked against the L-P-S formula. "S/U" grading is embedded at the end of the description text (Th and En) rather than shown as a separate grading note.
