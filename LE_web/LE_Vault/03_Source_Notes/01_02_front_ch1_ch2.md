## หมวดที่ 2 ความต้องการจำเป็นและข้อกำหนดที่สำคัญ
- **2.1 Stakeholder needs**
  - Learners want practical skills, hands-on tools and real problems. Parents want quality and employability. Employers want job-readiness, problem solving, tech use and cross-disciplinary teamwork. Government and community want engineering support for the local economy.
  - IE competencies: design and improve production and work systems; DOE; data analysis; OR; automation; project management; feasibility; innovation. Cost, quality, safety and life-cycle are considered throughout.
  - SCM competencies: material, product and information flow; integrated logistics design; demand and supply planning; inventory, warehouse, transport and distribution; quantitative models; IS and digital tools; cost, risk and sustainability.
  - Shared competencies: complex problem solving; Thai/English communication; teamwork and leadership; law, ethics and safety; lifelong learning.
- **2.2 Policy alignment**
  - National Strategy 2561–2580, and the master plan under issue 7 (infrastructure, logistics, digital)
  - NESDP 13 (2566–2570): หมุดหมาย 5 (logistics gateway), 1 (agri and food processing), 12 (high-skill workforce)
  - Thai Logistics Development Action Plan 2566–2570
  - Transport and logistics action plan, phase 1 (2566–2570) and phase 2 (2571–2580)
  - Rail freight plan of the Department of Rail Transport
  - MOAC agri-logistics plan 2566–2570
  - BCG plan 2564–2570
  - MHESI / สอวช. workforce studies
  - กฎกระทรวงมาตรฐานหลักสูตร 2565 and มาตรฐานคุณวุฒิ 2565; AUN-QA; TABEE
- **2.3 Vision, mission, identity**
  - Context is Kalasin and the Northeast: agriculture, agri-processing, trade and transport.
  - Delivery runs through the philosophy of experiential learning plus CWIE.
  - Identity is mapped as: มุ่งมั่น = responsibility and persistence; สร้างสรรค์ = solution and process design; เชี่ยวชาญวิชาชีพ = correct use of engineering tools and ethical decisions. These are tied to the OBE/PLO framework.
- **2.4 External risks**
  - Climate, disasters, energy prices and environmental rules → resilient, sustainable systems
  - Geopolitics and trade, supply-chain disruption → data and model-based decisions, risk and resilience
  - Digital, automation, AI, cybersecurity and skilled-labour shortage → transferable fundamentals and self-learning
  - Shrinking student cohorts and competition between universities → track labour-market trends, update courses, industry partnership
  - All of these feed into the PLO design and review.
